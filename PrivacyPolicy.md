# Cash Flow Privacy Policy — Draft

**Effective date:** November 8, 2026  
**Release status:** Draft. Add the developer’s real contact details and publish this document at a public URL before App Store submission.

Cash Flow is a personal cash-flow tracking app. This policy describes the current app implementation. Update it if the app’s data practices change.

## Information in the app

Cash Flow stores the information you enter—opening cash, income, expenses, descriptions, categories, dates, and pay periods—in the app’s local data directory on your Apple device. The app does not provide accounts, connect to banks, or send ledger data to the developer’s or another provider’s server. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Sharing and device backups

If you choose **Export ledger backup**, Cash Flow creates a JSON copy and opens Apple’s system sharing interface. The location or service you choose may store or transmit that copy according to its own terms and privacy practices. Cash Flow does not receive a copy back from that destination.

iOS device backups may include app data according to your device backup settings and Apple’s backup behavior.

## Security

The app’s ledger file uses iOS Complete Data Protection. iOS keeps the file encrypted on disk and makes it inaccessible to the app while the device is locked. An exported backup is outside the app’s protection; choose a destination you trust.

Optional app lock uses Apple’s LocalAuthentication system. Cash Flow does not receive or store Face ID or Touch ID data.

## Retention and deletion

Your ledger stays in the app until you delete it. Use **Settings → Delete all local data** to erase the ledger from the app, or delete Cash Flow from your device. This does not delete backups or copies you exported to another location; remove those separately.
